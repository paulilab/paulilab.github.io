Theresa obtained a BSc in Biology from the [University of Cologne](https://www.uni-koeln.de/en/), Germany, in 2022. For her Bachelor’s thesis, she joined the lab of [Ana Garcia-Saez](https://www.cecad.uni-koeln.de/research/principal-investigators/full-members/ana-j-garcia-saez) at the CECAD in Cologne where she worked on mitochondria-ER contact site modulators. In 2023, Theresa began her Master’s in Molecular Biology with a focus on stem cell and developmental biology at the [University of Vienna](https://www.univie.ac.at/en/). She first joined the Pauli lab for an internship in summer 2024 and returned in February 2025 for her Master’s thesis. After a half a year break, Theresa came back to join us as a shared technician with Diana Pinheiro's lab to help with genotyping fish. In her free time, Theresa enjoys reading, sports (tennis and running) and traveling.
