**Mia** is an undergraduate Biology student at the Universidad Peruana de Ciencia Aplicadas in Lima, Peru. She joins the Pauli lab for 3 months under the Talents-for-Future program, a part of the VBC summer school. In Lima, Mia works in the lab of [Prof. Pohl Milón](https://www.sinapsis-peru.org/copy-of-dr-catriona-fyffe) to untangle the dynamics of translational initiation in E. coli. 
In Vienna, she will be expanding her horizons into eukaryotic translation, studying the mechanisms of Poly-A Independent Non-canonical Translation (PAINT) in early zebrafish embryogenesis with Ezra. Welcome Mia!
