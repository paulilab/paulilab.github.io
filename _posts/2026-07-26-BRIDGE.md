Big congratulations to **David** and **Jörg** on the publication of their BRIDGE [paper](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/42/7/btag558/8743603?login=true). 
David joined the Pauli lab for a computational internship supervised by Jörg in 2025 as part of his bioinformatics Bachelor studies at [Universitat Pompeu Fabra](https://www.esci.upf.edu/en), and continued to work with Jörg on this paper over the past year from Spain. 
If you have large multi-omics datasets (transcriptomics, proteomics and phospho-proteomics) that you would like to explore without prior computational expertise, check out our versatile [BRIDGE shiny web application](https://ghcr.io/paulilab/bridge), which allows exploration and easy plotting of 'omics' datasets.
A public demonstration server with example datasets across early zebrafish development is available [here](https://bridge.imp.ac.at). Code and datasets are also available on [github](https://github.com/paulilab/BRIDGE) and [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20215824).
Congrats to all authors!
