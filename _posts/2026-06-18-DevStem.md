Pauli group members **Muriel**, **Mia**, **Theresa**, **Tobias**, **Sebastian** and **Ezra** ventured out to [ISTA](https://www.ista.ac.at/home) this week for the annual [DevStem symposium](https://devstem.ist.ac.at/). For Muriel, Tobias and Ezra neither the summer heat nor the inclination could deter them from cycling all the way along the Danube to Klosterneuburg. 
The symposium featured a broad range of talks from axolotl limb regeneration to the control of cell fate by metabolism, interspersed with poster sessions, during which Theresa, Sebastian and Ezra presented their work, and of course food and coffee! 
A special shout out to **Mia** for attending her first conference and **Theresa** for presenting a great flash talk!
